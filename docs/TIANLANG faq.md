# TIANLANG Lightbox - Frequently Asked Questions

## English FAQ

### Product Questions

**Q1: What is a SEG Lightbox?**
A: SEG (Silicone Edge Graphics) lightbox features a frameless design with silicone edges that stretch into a channel on the aluminum frame, creating a perfectly flat, wrinkle-free display surface. It allows for quick graphic changes in under 3 seconds.

**Q2: What sizes do you offer?**
A: We offer standard sizes (85*200cm,100*200cm,100*300cm,200*300cm,285*300cm,300*300cm, etc.) and full customization up to 6000×4000mm. Any size, any shape (straight or curved) is available.

**Q3: Can you customize shapes?**
A: Yes. We support straight, curved, circular, and irregular shapes. Custom shapes are designed and manufactured to your specifications.

**Q4: What printing technology do you use?**
A: We use sublimation printing (8-color eco-friendly), roll-to-roll printing, and 5M UV double-sided printing at 1440dpi resolution.

**Q5: What is the LED lifespan?**
A: Our 2835 SMD LED strips have a lifespan of 50,000+ hours.

**Q6: Do you offer OEM/ODM services?**
A: Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM services with in-house production from structure to graphics. Custom branding, packaging, and labeling are available.

**Q7: What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?**
A: No MOQ for standard products. Custom projects are welcome regardless of quantity.
