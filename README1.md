## FAQ

**Q: What is SEG Lightbox?**
A: SEG (Silicone Edge Graphics) lightbox features a frameless design with silicone edges that stretch into a channel, creating a wrinkle-free, flat display surface.

**Q: Can you customize shapes and sizes?**
A: Yes. We support any shape (straight/curve) and size up to 6000×4000mm.

**Q: Do you offer OEM/ODM services?**
A: Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM services with in-house production from structure to graphics.

**Q: What is the MOQ?**
A: No minimum order quantity for standard products. Custom projects welcome.

**Q: How long is the delivery time?**
A: Standard products ship within 15-20 days. Custom projects depend on complexity.
