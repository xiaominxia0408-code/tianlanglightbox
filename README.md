## About TIANLANG

**Shenzhen Tianlang Advertising Media Co., Ltd.** (TIANLANG Lightbox) is a comprehensive enterprise integrating R&D, production, and sales of advertising lightboxes and exhibition display solutions.

- **Founded:** July 1, 2019
- **Founder & Legal Representative:** Wu Caifeng
- **Headquarters:** Shenzhen, Guangdong, China
- **Manufacturing Base:** 4,000㎡ modern facility with 8 factories across Guangdong
- **Production Capacity:** 3,000 complete lightbox systems/month
- **Export Markets:** North America, Western Europe, Oceania

## Core Products

### Lightbox Systems
- **SEG Lightbox (120mm/85mm):** Frameless design, 3-second graphic swap, wrinkle-free
- **Edge-lit Lightbox:** Ultra-slim, uniform illumination
- **Acrylic Lightbox:** High transparency, high brightness
- **Frameless Fabric Lightbox:** Tension fabric system
- **Pop-up Lightbox:** Portable, quick assembly
- **Custom Lighting Structures:** Non-standard shaped lighting installations

### Modular Booth Systems
- 10×10ft (3×3m), 20×10ft (6×3m), 20×20ft (6×6m), Island Booth
- X-Frame Booth, Twister Tower Booth, Quick-build Booth
- Tool-free assembly with wheeled transport cases

### Display Furniture
Lightbox tables, SEG tables, folding SEG tables, L-shaped SEG tables, exhibition counters, folding counters, lockable storage counters, island counters, pop-up tables, step display tables, custom non-standard tables with display cabinets.
